# NEXUS Demo Checklist

- [ ] Backend `/api/health` reports Hindsight bank ready and Groq configured.
- [ ] Frontend opens without console errors.
- [ ] Click **Analyze with NEXUS**.
- [ ] Hindsight panel shows at least one recalled evidence item.
- [ ] Decision explains a successful prior approach and an avoided failed approach.
- [ ] Enter resolution, technical result and business outcome.
- [ ] Click **Resolve & Teach NEXUS**.
- [ ] Open **Experience Library** and show the retained outcome.
- [ ] Load Demo Incident 2.
- [ ] Analyze again and show the new decision using organizational memory.

## Suggested narration

“NEXUS does not start from a blank prompt. It first recalls what the organization has already learned. It remembers what worked, what failed, who the customer is and what the business outcome was. After a human confirms the actual outcome, that experience is retained and becomes evidence for the next incident.”
